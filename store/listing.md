## Title

SwidScanner: Document Scanner

## Short description

Scan documents to PDF with live page detection. Offline, no ads, no account.

## Full description

SwidScanner turns your phone into a document scanner. Point the camera at a page and it outlines the edges as you aim, crops and straightens the shot, cleans up the colours and saves it as a PDF. Everything happens on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

SCAN
- Live page outline while you aim
- Auto-capture once the page is steady, or tap the shutter yourself
- Single mode for one page, batch mode for a stack
- Book mode: one photo of an open book becomes a left and a right page
- ID card mode: front and back on one A4 page at true size, ready to print
- Import photos or PDFs from your phone and get the same automatic crop
- Optional Google scanner mode, if you prefer Google's camera screens

CLEAN UP
- Drag the corners to adjust the crop, with a magnifier
- Filters: Original, Magic (flattens shadows and whitens paper), Enhance, Grayscale and B&W
- Rotate, delete, reorder and select pages

SIGN AND STAMP
- Keep a library of signatures and stamps
- Draw a signature or use a photo
- Add text stamps such as APPROVED and PAID
- Move, resize, rotate and fade them on any page

READ
- Text recognition (OCR) that works offline
- Copy, edit or share the text
- Searchable PDFs with a hidden text layer
- QR code and barcode scanner

SAVE AND SHARE
- Export as PDF, JPEG images or a text file
- PDF options: Fit image, A4 or Letter, three quality levels and an optional password
- Save to a folder on your phone or SD card
- Optionally save to your own Google Drive. SwidScanner can only see the files it creates
- Auto-save every document a few seconds after you scan or edit it
- Share to any app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account and no ads
- No analytics and no crash reporting
- Scans, text recognition and PDF creation never leave your phone
- The only network use is the Google Drive upload, and only if you link it

OPEN SOURCE
SwidScanner is free and open source under the MIT License. Source code and issue tracker: https://github.com/SwikrutD/SwidScanner

Requires Android 8.0 or later.
