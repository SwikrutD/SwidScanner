SwidScanner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

SwidScanner is an open-source document scanner for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • SwidScanner has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.
  • Scans, text recognition and PDF creation all happen on your device.
  • The only network traffic is to Google's Drive service, and only if you link Google Drive.

Data the app handles on your device

When you scan a page, the app stores the photos, the cropped pages, the text recognized from them and the document names inside its private app storage. This data stays on your device. It is removed when you delete a document in the app or uninstall the app.

Text recognition (OCR) uses an offline model bundled with the app. Page detection, filters, barcode reading and PDF export also run on the device.

Camera and photos

SwidScanner asks for camera permission so it can scan pages and barcodes. It can also import photos you choose from your gallery. Images are only read when you pick them. The app does not upload them anywhere.

Saving to folders you choose

If you use the Save button or Auto-save, the app writes PDF files to a folder you select on your phone or SD card. Those files are yours and are not sent to the developer.

Google Drive (optional)

You can link Google Drive in Settings. The app then uses Google's official sign-in and asks for the drive.file permission. With that permission the app can only see files and folders it created itself.

When you save to Drive, the app uploads PDF files to a SwidScanner folder in your Google Drive. Those uploads go directly from your phone to Google. The developer never sees them. Google handles your data under its own Privacy Policy.

You can unlink Google Drive at any time in Settings. You can also revoke access in your Google Account under third-party access.

Permissions

  • Camera: scanning pages and barcodes.
  • Internet: used only to upload to Google Drive when you link it.

Backups

Android may back up app data to your Google account as part of its standard device backup. This is controlled by Android and your device settings, not by the developer.

Children

SwidScanner does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
